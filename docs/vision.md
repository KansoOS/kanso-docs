# Vision

KansoOS helps neuroatypical users gain autonomy on smartphones by reducing cognitive load, limiting distractions, and allowing trusted caregivers to configure essential usage rules remotely.

The project focuses on accessibility, autonomy, safety, and maintainability. It is not positioned as a medical device for the MVP and does not diagnose, treat, or monitor medical conditions.

## Product Principles

- Keep the user interface predictable and low-distraction.
- Give the user access to essential actions first.
- Make caregiver control explicit, auditable, and revocable.
- Preserve compatibility with certified Android devices whenever possible.
- Avoid deep OS customization unless it provides clear product value.

## Target Users

- Neuroatypical users who need a simpler and more predictable smartphone environment.
- Family caregivers who help configure and maintain the device.
- Institutions or support professionals who may manage multiple devices later.

## Non-Goals For MVP

- Building a complete Android fork as the main product path.
- Claiming medical effectiveness.
- Replacing emergency services or medical supervision.
- Supporting every Android device from day one.
